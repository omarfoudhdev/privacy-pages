03Information we collect

Radio [Country] Pro apps collect only the technical information needed to run the streaming service and improve it:

CategoryExamplesPurpose
Device & OS dataOS version, device model, app versionCompatibility, crash fixing
Usage dataStations played, session length, crash/error logsReliability, feature improvement
Advertising / analytics identifiersAnonymized or resettable ad ID (where applicable)Ad delivery and aggregate analytics

We do not require account creation and we do not knowingly collect names, precise location, contacts, or other sensitive personal data to operate the core radio-streaming feature.

04How we use information

  • Operating and maintaining live audio streaming
  • Diagnosing and fixing bugs or crashes
  • Understanding aggregate, anonymized usage to prioritize improvements
  • Serving ads through third-party ad networks, where the app includes ads

05Sharing of information

We do not sell personal data. We do not share personally identifiable information with third parties for their own marketing purposes. Advertising and analytics partners integrated into the apps receive only anonymized or aggregated data, consistent with their own privacy policies and applicable law.

06Data retention & your choices

Technical and usage data is retained only as long as necessary for the purposes above. You can limit ad personalization through your device's system settings (e.g. "Opt out of Ads Personalization" on Android), and you can stop all data collection at any time by uninstalling the app.

07Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information we collect against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

08Children's privacy

Radio [Country] Pro applications are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

09Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page always reflects the latest version, and it applies immediately to every app in the Radio [Country] Pro family upon publication.
